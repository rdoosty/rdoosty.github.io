**AReS** (Connectivity and Situational Awareness via AI-Enabled ISAC)
aims to help first responders assess damaged environments, restore and
adapt wireless connectivity, localize people and assets, and deliver
real-time situational awareness. By enabling wireless networks to sense
their surroundings as well as communicate, AReS supports more informed
and coordinated disaster recovery.
