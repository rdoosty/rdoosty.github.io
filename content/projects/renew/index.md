**RENEW** (Reconfigurable Eco-system for Next-generation End-to-end
Wireless) is an open-source, software-defined massive MIMO platform
deployed on the NSF PAWR/POWDER testbed. RENEW provides researchers
with programmable access to large-scale antenna arrays and end-to-end
PHY/MAC stacks for at-scale wireless experimentation. Funded by NSF
PAWR, April 2018 – March 2023.
