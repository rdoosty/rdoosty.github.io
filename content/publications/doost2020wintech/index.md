abstract: |
  We argue that wireless research is entering an exceptional era, driven
  by the convergence of open, programmable hardware (software-defined
  radios, white-box base stations), large-scale shared testbeds (PAWR,
  POWDER, COSMOS, AERPAW), and open-source 5G/6G software stacks. We
  reflect on lessons learned from the RENEW massive MIMO platform and
  outline opportunities for the wireless community to leverage these
  resources for reproducible, at-scale experimentation.

summary: |
  A position paper on the new opportunities for wireless research enabled
  by open testbeds, programmable radios, and open-source 5G software.
