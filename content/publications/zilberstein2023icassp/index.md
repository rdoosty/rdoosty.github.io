abstract: |
  We present an accelerated massive MIMO detector based on annealed
  underdamped Langevin dynamics. By introducing a momentum term and a
  carefully tuned annealing schedule, the detector converges in
  significantly fewer iterations than the overdamped variant while
  preserving near-optimal symbol error performance, making it suitable
  for high-throughput baseband implementations.
