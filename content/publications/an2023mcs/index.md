abstract: |
  Adaptive modulation and coding scheme (MCS) selection is critical for
  link efficiency in massive multi-user MIMO. We propose a machine
  learning-based MCS selector that operates without explicit user
  feedback, using base-station-side observations of channel statistics
  and traffic to predict the optimal MCS per user. The proposed scheme
  improves throughput and reduces signaling overhead compared with
  conventional feedback-based approaches.

summary: |
